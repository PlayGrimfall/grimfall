Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Grimfall collects, why, and what we do with it. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Guests: nothing personal. A guest's hero is saved only in their own browser.
  • Account holders: an email address, a password (stored scrambled, so we can't read it), a player name, an age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older), and the saved hero with its level, gear and gold.
  • Children under 13: the account uses the parent's email address. From the child we collect only the player name, which should not be their real name, and their saved hero. The parent's chat settings and a scrambled copy of the parent PIN are stored with the account.

What we don't collect

  • We don't ask for real names, addresses, phone numbers or photos.
  • Typed chat messages are shown on screen for a few seconds and are not stored.
  • Voice chat goes directly between players' browsers and is not recorded.
  • We don't show ads and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use account information only to sign players in, save their heroes, apply the chat settings a parent chose, and answer emails. We don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't share it except with the services that run the game, listed below.

Services that help run the game

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved heroes.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • PeerJS helps players' browsers find each other for online play and voice. It sees the connection's internet address but not chat or voice.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code files to the browser.

Like any website, these services can see basic technical details such as an internet address and browser type when the page loads.

Children's privacy

Children under 13 can only get an account through a parent or guardian, who sets it up with their own email address. Parents can see and change the chat settings with their PIN, and can ask us at any time to review or delete their child's information or to stop collecting it. We'll delete it promptly once we confirm the request comes from the account's email address.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep account information until the account is deleted. To delete an account and its saved hero, email us from the account's email address and we'll remove both.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects children's information, we'll ask parents again before it applies.
