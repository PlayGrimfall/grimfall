Washed up in Sumera

The tide carried you in at dawn, holding on to a broken plank. Old Maren, the harbor keeper, finds you on the dock and tells you what happened to her town.

The Lich Queen woke beneath the castle. Her dead fill the halls below, and every night they creep a little closer to the houses.

“You hold that weapon like you know how to use it. Maybe the sea brought you to us for a reason.”Old Maren, the harbor keeper

Choose your hero

Each hero fights in their own way. Level up, spend skill points, and dress them in armor you find deep in the castle.

Necromancer

Dark magic from afar. Calls on the dead.

  • Soul Fire hurls green flame.
  • Raise Skeletons brings up three bony fighters with red eyes.

Archer

Arrows from far away, a knife up close.

  • Triple Shot fires three arrows at once.
  • Arrow Storm rains arrows on a crowd.

Barbarian

An armored druid with twin axes.

  • Earthquake shakes the ground around you.
  • Bear Rage makes you faster and much stronger.

Healer

Keeps the party alive and fires a holy beam.

  • Arcane Bolt pierces through enemies.
  • Healing Light heals you in a burst.

Down into the castle

Follow the forest path east to the castle gate. Four floors of halls, traps and locked doors lie between you and the Lich Queen.

Bosses

Every floor ends with a boss guarding the way down.

Armor sets

Collect all four pieces of your hero's set, or match their colors, for big bonuses.

Crystals

Set rubies, diamonds and fairy amber into your gear for extra powers.

Play together

One player hosts a room and gets a four-letter code. Friends type the code and join the same town and the same dungeon, up to four heroes at once.

WOLF

Talk with quick-chat or typed chat, which filters bad words. Voice chat works too, unless a parent has it turned off.

For parents

Grimfall is made for older kids and teens. The fights are fantasy battles with no blood: defeated monsters simply fade away.

  • Kids under 13 need a parent to make their account, using the parent's email.
  • Under-13 players only get preset quick-chat phrases. You can turn typed chat and voice on with your own PIN.
  • Grimfall is free to play and has no purchases right now.
