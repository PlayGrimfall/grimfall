A guide for parents

Everything you need to know before your child plays Grimfall.

What Grimfall is

Grimfall is a free fantasy adventure that runs in a web browser. Players pick a hero, explore a small town, and fight monsters in the dungeons under a castle. It's made for older kids and teens. The fights have no blood: defeated monsters simply fade away.

Playing without an account

Anyone can play as a guest. A guest's hero is saved only in that browser on that computer. We don't collect any personal information from guests.

Accounts and ages

An account saves a hero online so it can be played on any computer. When someone makes an account, the game asks their age first:

  • Under 13: a parent or guardian has to set up the account with the parent's own email address. The child only picks a player name, and we ask that it isn't their real name. The parent also sets a 4-number PIN for the chat settings.
  • 13 to 17: the player makes the account and confirms that a parent or guardian knows about it.
  • 18 or older: the player makes the account themselves.

Chat and voice

Up to four players can play together by sharing a room code. Players can talk in these ways:

  • Quick-chat: preset phrases like “Follow me!” and “Need healing!”. This is the only chat players under 13 get, unless a parent turns on more.
  • Typed chat: short messages with a filter for bad words. Messages show on screen for a few seconds and are not stored anywhere.
  • Voice chat: players can choose to join voice. Audio goes straight between the players' browsers and is not recorded.

To change the chat settings for a child under 13, open the game, click Account, then Parent settings, and enter your PIN. There you can turn typed chat and voice chat on or off.

Only people who have your child's room code can join their game. Remind your child to share codes only with friends they know.

Money

Grimfall is free and has no purchases right now. If we add any in the future, we'll update this page first, and players under 18 will need a parent to approve.

Questions or deleting an account

Email us at playgrimfall@proton.me. To delete your child's account and saved hero, write to us from the parent email on the account and we'll remove it. You can also read our privacy policy.
